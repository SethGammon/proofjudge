The deployed judges

How a verdict becomes proof

  1. Terms + rubriclocked before judgment
  2. Submitted workhashed on arrival
  3. EigenCompute judgescored under an app identity
  4. Signed receiptverdict + hashes + signature
  5. Verify · tamper-test · settlebefore money moves

Verification proves

  • The receipt came from the deployed judge identity
  • No field changed after the verdict was signed
  • The hashes match the inputs that were judged

It does not prove

  • That the verdict is objectively correct
  • That the rubric itself was wise
  • Offline third-party HMAC validation (service-verifiable today)
